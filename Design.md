# Opus Magnum: Design Direction

A fast, movement-first arena shooter. Gunplay should feel tactile and weighty, movement fluid and
expressive. Everything on screen should read instantly.

## Feel
- **Movement first.** Sliding, vaulting and mantling flow into each other with momentum. Nothing should
  stop the player abruptly.
- **Tactile guns.** Every shot has weight: recoil you can see and feel, moving mechanical parts, and
  reloads that show real hand work.
- **Readable over realistic.** Exaggerate where it helps the feel, but keep it believable: hands grip
  properly and parts move the way they would.

## Visual style
- **Cel-shaded and stylised.** Toon shading in a few flat bands, ink outlines and a soft rim light. No noisy
  textures; form comes from shape and value.
- **Warm, restrained palette.** Ivory and sand surfaces, dark gunmetal and polymer, with clay orange as the
  signature accent. Colour is used sparingly so it means something.
- **Clean environments.** Simple geometric spaces with clear landmarks and grid floors. Clutter only where
  it serves play.

## Weapons
- **Big, confident shapes.** Each gun is built from a few chunky, rounded forms with real depth, not many
  tiny details. The silhouette should identify the gun at a glance.
- **One accent per gun.** Each weapon owns a distinct accent colour (clay, olive, sand, red) so they're
  easy to tell apart.
- **Consistent family.** Shared proportions, materials and construction logic, so the arsenal feels like
  it comes from one world.
- **First-person framing.** Guns sit low and to the right, angled toward the crosshair. They're present
  but never crowd the centre of the screen.

## Items
- **Same family as the guns.** Chunky rounded forms, ink outlines, one identifying colour each: red medkit,
  ivory bandage, cyan charger, clay-banded grenade, olive ammo box with brass showing.
- **Rarity is colour and shape.** Armour tiers go sand, blue, purple, and the chest badge shows one to three bars.
- **Readable on the floor.** Pickups hover over a soft ring in their colour and turn slowly; small items are
  shown larger than life.
- **Effects stay in style.** An explosion is one lumpy, ink-outlined fireball with banded, mottled fire that
  cools from pale yellow through orange and red into grey smoke — no particle soup.

## Interface
- **Neo-brutalist, but calm.** Solid ivory cards, thick dark borders and hard offset shadows, paired with
  an elegant, minimal layout and generous spacing.
- **Type with character.** A serif for names and titles, monospace for labels and numbers.
- **Quiet until needed.** The HUD stays out of the way; feedback (hits, ammo, state changes) is crisp and
  brief.

## Sound
- Punchy, physical weapon sounds; mechanical detail on reloads and handling.
- Footsteps and ambience stay subtle so combat audio stays clear.
- Free-licence (CC0) sources, credited.
